The running order of a live wedding

This is what a day with Nuvis On Air sounds like. Times are approximate: we adapt to your masia's schedule.

  1. −3 weeks

    Script call

    You tell us who is who: the grandmother who knows everything, the friend who never stops talking, the cousin who lives abroad. With that we prepare the questions and the order of the show.

  2. 5:45 pm

    Quiet set-up

    We arrive before the guests and build the set in a 3 × 3 metre corner with one power socket: two armchairs, microphones, cameras and warm light. It is ready in 45 minutes.

  3. 6:30 pm

    The light goes on

    The drinks reception begins. The host invites the people you named, and anyone who fancies it, to step up to the set. About ten minutes per group, with headphones and question cards.

  4. 9:00 pm

    Over dinner

    During dinner we record the calmer segments: the grandparents' table, the advice nobody asked for and the message for ten years from now.

  5. 11:30 pm

    Last voices

    With the party in full swing, the set fills up with friends. These are usually the funniest recordings of the night.

  6. +72 h

    The preview

    We send you a one-minute clip to share with the family while you finish unpacking your suitcases.

  7. +4 weeks

    The episode

    You receive the edited episode, the vertical clips and a private link to listen on Spotify or Apple Podcasts.

The segments of the show

The host guides the conversation with regular segments. That way nobody goes blank and the episode has rhythm.

  • The grandparents' table

    Stories from the old days, told by the people who lived them.

  • What you did not know

    Anecdotes your friends have been saving up for years.

  • Advice nobody asked for

Frequently asked questions

What if our guests are shy?

That is normal in the first few minutes. The host breaks the ice with simple questions, cards and humour. Nobody is recorded unless they want to be, and once the first group has been through, the others queue up.

How much space do you need?

A corner of about 3 × 3 metres with one power socket, away from the DJ's speakers. We set up in 45 minutes before the guests arrive.

What happens if it rains?

We set up indoors. The set works just as well in a stone hall or under a marquee; we agree this beforehand with your masia.

Which language is the recording in?

Catalan, Spanish or English. The host switches language depending on the guest, and the episode can carry subtitles.

Who can listen to the episode?

Only the people you choose. We give you a private podcast link for you and up to 50 more people, each with their own access, which you can withdraw whenever you like.

How do you protect your guests' privacy?

We put up an information notice next to the set and only those who wish to are recorded. We publish nothing on our social media without your permission and that of the people who appear. With minors, only with their parents' permission.

When do we receive the episode?

The preview within 72 hours and the full episode in about four weeks. With express delivery, in ten days.

How do we book?

A 30 % deposit of the pack price secures the date. The balance is paid ten days before the wedding by bank transfer or Bizum.

Do you work outside Catalonia?

Yes. We send you a quote with travel included.

Can we choose who you interview?

Yes. On the script call you tell us who must not be missed and which stories you want to hear.
